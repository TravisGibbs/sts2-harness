# Ironclad playbook (ascension 0)

Checked against this game's code and recorded runs; it is not the first Slay the Spire. Follow it where it is more specific than the advice above; trust the screen over both.

Bosses are on floors 17, 33 and 48, each after a rest site; every act starts at full HP.

## Damage per turn decides Act 1
- Estimate it: your attacks' total damage, divided by deck size, times 5 (cards drawn per turn).
- Against the Waterfall Giant, recorded decks under 20 a turn lost 6 of 6; 20+ entering at 50+ HP won 4 of 4.
- Until you reach 20, take the attack that adds most (Bludgeon, Uppercut, Pommel Strike, Twin Strike, Hemokinesis, Dismantle with Vulnerable) over block cards.
- Then build one engine. Strength: Demon Form, Inflame, Rupture, paid off by Twin Strike, Sword Boomerang, Whirlwind. Vulnerable: Bash, Tremble, Thunderclap, Uppercut, Taunt; Molten Fist doubles it; Bully, Dismantle, Cruelty cash it in. Block: Barricade, Unmovable, Body Slam, Juggernaut.
- Traps: Anger floods long fights; Perfected Strike weakens as Strikes go; Greed (Unplayable, Eternal) is worth Sunken Treasury's 310 gold only with a shop ahead.

## Map
- `info paths` at every fork: "then Rest" shows one room. On one seed both floor-8 rooms said "then Rest"; one led to two forced elites and no shop, the other to no elite, a shop and three rests. All 18 runs there took the first.
- Fight an Act 1 elite only at 70%+ HP, with 20+ damage a turn and a rest site after it; otherwise take the lowest Elite count. Recorded Act 1 elites cost a median 19-28 HP.
- With 150+ gold, route through a shop. Buy a damage card, then remove a Defend (or a Strike once replaced; 75 gold, +25 each time), then a potion.

## Act 1 elites (`info enemy` on turn 1)
- Terror Eel, 140 HP. At 70 or below it is Stunned once, then Terrorizes (you get 99 Vulnerable), then Crashes for 16, +6 after Thrash, x1.5 = 33. Crossing 70 skips its attacks that turn and the next: save potions and big attacks for the last 70. Decks under 15 a turn lost 4 of 6.
- Skulking Colony, 75 HP, loses at most 20 HP a turn. Block the two 14s.
- Phantasmal Gardeners: four of 26-31 HP, each gaining Block when first hit each turn. Use area and multi-hit attacks.

## Act 1 bosses (`info boss`)
- Waterfall Giant, 240 HP, killed 9 of 13 recorded runs that reached it. Steam Eruption starts at 15 and grows 3 a turn (30 on turn 7, 42 on turn 11). Killing it freezes the count; it skips its next attack, then Explodes for the count at the end of your following turn. Block reduces it.
  - It is a race: each turn costs 3 eruption plus about 12 damage. Attack every turn, block only what would hurt, keep it Vulnerable.
  - Kill it once your HP plus one turn of block beats the count. Two runs held it under 45 HP for turns and died to 51 and 57.
  - On the eruption turn play every block card and block potion; attacks do nothing.
- Vantom: Slippery makes your first 8 damaging hits deal 1; strip it with cheap multi-hits. Block turn 3 (26).
- Ceremonial Beast: +2 Strength a turn until it drops to 150 HP, which stuns it. Race there.
- The Kin: killing the Kin Priest (190 HP) ends the fight.
- Soul Fysh: play each Beckon or lose 6 HP. While it is Intangible, block and set up.
- Lagavulin Matriarch sleeps three turns: play Powers, then open big. It drains Strength and Dexterity.

## Later bosses
- The Insatiable: Sandpit 0 kills you; play Frantic Escape whenever Sandpit is 3 or less.
- Knowledge Demon heals and gains Strength every fourth turn; avoid the Waste Away curse.
- Kaiser Crab: kill both bodies together; the survivor gains Strength.
- Queen: after turn 1 only one of the first three cards drawn is playable; after turn 2 you are Weak, Frail and Vulnerable for good. Powers on turn 1; kill the Torch Head Amalgam first.
- Test Subject has three lives (100, 200, 300 HP); Aeonglass's Artifact eats your first three debuffs.

## Rest sites and potions
- Rest heals 30% of max HP. Above 70% HP, Smith your engine or best attack; below, rest before an elite or boss.
- Use potions in every elite and boss fight; 4 of 9 Giant deaths held one unused.
